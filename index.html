Where should your next dollar go?

Three popular money plans answer that question in different orders. Answer a few questions and see what each one would do with your money, side by side.

  • Dave Ramsey's Baby StepsSeven steps. Pay off every debt except the house before you invest.
  • The Money Guy's Financial Order of OperationsNine steps. Take free money first, and treat debt differently depending on its rate and your age.
  • The Reddit flowchartA community-built chart from r/personalfinance and r/financialindependence. Many branches, each tied to your situation.

None of them is the right one. Each makes sense for someone, and seeing them together shows you where they agree and where you have a real choice to make.
